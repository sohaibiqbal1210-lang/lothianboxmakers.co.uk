Pick the right board

ECT stands for edge crush test. It measures how much weight the board takes before the wall buckles. If you stack on pallets, this is the number that matters.

B Flute

Single wall, 3mm

5.2kN/m ECT

Burst strength 925 kPa

Postal, retail and light goods up to about 10kg.

BE Flute

Double wall, 5mm

8.7kN/m ECT

Burst strength 1,350 kPa

Moving, stacking and heavy goods from 15kg. This is the one most people order.

BC Flute

Double wall, 7mm

8.2kN/m ECT

Burst strength 1,416 kPa

Heavy duty work, fragile contents and export.
